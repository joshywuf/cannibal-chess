How to play

Cannibal Chess follows standard chess rules, with one addition.

  1. You can capture your own pieces. Any piece can take a friendly piece that it could normally move onto. Pawns capture friendly pieces diagonally.
  2. Your king can’t eat your pieces. It can only capture enemy pieces. Nobody can capture a king, and you can’t make a move that leaves your king in check.
  3. It costs your turn. A self-capture is a full move, shown in the move list with ⊗ and a red ring on the board.
  4. Everything else is normal: castling, en passant, checkmate, stalemate. Pawns that reach the last rank become queens.

Opponents: Lexi (King Leap), Rober (Double Jump) and Arthur (Horse Form) each have a special move shown beside the board. Beat one, then harvest their power or spare them. A spared opponent helps you later with one use of their move.

Ideas to try: eat your own blocker to open a line to the enemy king, or give up a piece to free a better one.
